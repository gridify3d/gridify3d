Features
Everything you need
to create stunning structures
A full browser-based tool — no installation, no plugins, just upload and generate.
🔷

Wire mode

Diamond, hexagon, square, triangle, diagonal weave — each precisely projected spherically onto any 3D surface.

🥚

Surface mode

Punch or stamp shapes (cylinder, star, heart, flower) through a shell surface for unique decorative objects.

🎯

Exclusion Zones

Paint exclusion zones directly in the 3D preview. Marked regions are rendered as solid shells instead of wireframe.

📐

Full scale control

Scale X, Y, Z independently. Live preview updates instantly — what you see is what you print.

⚡

Queue system

Multiple users can generate simultaneously. Jobs are queued fairly and run in parallel with live progress.

💾

Save & load projects

All settings, zones, and even the STL are saved in a single project file. Reopen and continue any time.

⭕

Open bottom & floor cut

Optionally cut a clean floor with or without a rim so your model stands flat straight off the printer. Wire and punch mode leave the bottom open automatically — ideal for tea lights or decorative inserts.

How it works
From STL to print-ready
in three steps
1

Upload your STL

Drag and drop any watertight STL file. Gridify3D loads it instantly in the 3D preview — rotate, inspect, and confirm your model looks right.

2

Configure & paint zones

Pick your pattern, adjust density and tube radius. Optionally paint Exclusion Zones directly on the mesh to turn specific areas into solid surfaces.

3

Generate & download

Hit Generate. Your job runs on the server and you follow live progress in the log. When done, download the STL and slice it — ready for your 3D printer.
