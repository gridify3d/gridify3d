How it works
From STL to print-ready
in three steps
1

Upload your STL

Drag and drop any watertight STL file. Gridify3D loads it instantly in the 3D preview — rotate, inspect, and confirm your model looks right.

2

Configure & paint zones

Pick your pattern, adjust density and tube radius. Optionally paint Shell Zones directly on the mesh to get solid surfaces or different patterns in specific areas.

3

Generate & download

Hit Generate. Your job runs on the server and you follow live progress in the log. When done, download the STL and slice it — ready for your 3D printer.
